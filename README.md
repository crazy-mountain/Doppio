# Doppio

A double shot for your Mac.

Doppio keeps your Mac awake when you need it. Toggle it from the menu bar: an empty glass means off, a filled glass means on. Launch at login and start switched on are optional.

## Download

[Download the signed Doppio beta](https://github.com/crazy-mountain/Doppio/releases/tag/v0.1.1-beta.1). Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later.

Open the disk image, drag Doppio to Applications, then open it from Applications.
